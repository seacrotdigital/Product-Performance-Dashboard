# Product Performance Dashboard 📊

An interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze product performance, customer engagement, pricing strategies, stock availability, and review behavior across different product categories and brands.

---

# 📌 Dashboard Overview

This project provides a complete business intelligence solution for monitoring:
- Product popularity
- Ratings & customer sentiment
- Discounts & pricing impact
- Stock health
- Product growth trends
- Brand performance

The dashboard enables stakeholders to make data-driven decisions regarding inventory, pricing strategy, and product optimization.

---

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Products with higher discounts tend to attract higher popularity scores.
- Fashion products generated the highest average popularity.
- Apple recorded the highest total review volume.
- Products with very high popularity show increased stock-out risk.
- Most products fall within the “Good Rating” range (4–5 stars).
- Stock distribution is relatively balanced between In Stock, Limited Stock, and Out of Stock categories.
